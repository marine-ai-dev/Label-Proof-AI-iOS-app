LabelProof — Support

LabelProof is a free, on-device packaging-label verifier for iOS: scan or import a photo of a package and check it against a reference "golden label" — product name, weight, barcode, and required phrases — entirely on your device, with no account and no internet connection required.

Frequently asked questions

The camera scan isn't finding anything on the label.

Make sure the printed text and barcode are well-lit, in focus, and fill enough of the frame. You can also use "Import Photo" to verify a clearer photo taken separately.

Why does it say "Insufficient data" instead of PASS or FAIL?

This means the scan didn't produce enough readable text or a barcode to compare against your golden label — LabelProof reports this honestly rather than guessing at a result. Try rescanning with better lighting or focus.

Does LabelProof need an internet connection?

No. All recognition and verification happens on your device using Apple's Vision framework. LabelProof has no server and makes no network requests at all.

Where is my data stored?

Golden labels and verification history are stored only in local app storage on your device. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete an individual golden label or history entry with swipe-to-delete, or clear all history from Settings. Deleting the app itself also removes all its local data.

I denied camera access by mistake. What now?

You can still verify labels with "Import Photo." To re-enable the camera, go to iOS Settings → LabelProof → Camera and turn it on.

Report a bug or a security/privacy concern

Please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or for a security/privacy vulnerability specifically, follow the private disclosure process described in the repository's SECURITY.md (a GitHub Security Advisory, rather than a public issue).
