LabelProof — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

LabelProof is designed to be privacy-first by construction, not by policy alone. This page describes exactly what the app does and does not do — verified against its actual source code, not just asserted.

Summary

LabelProof collects no data of any kind. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection anywhere in the app. Everything you create in LabelProof — golden labels, verification history, and your appearance settings — stays only on your device.

What LabelProof does NOT do

  • No account, no login, no user identity of any kind.
  • No network requests — there is no networking code anywhere in the app: no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising SDK, no cloud service of any kind.
  • No cloud OCR or cloud AI. All text and barcode recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework, running entirely on your device.
  • No full images are stored. Only the extracted text lines, decoded barcode values, and the verification result are kept — never the photo or camera frame itself.

Permissions

Camera is requested only to scan a packaging label with your device's camera. Recognition happens entirely on-device; no image is ever uploaded or shared. If you decline, you can still verify labels by importing a photo from your Photos library instead.

Deleting your data

You can delete an individual golden label, an individual history entry, or clear all history at any time from within the app — these are real, immediate deletions from local storage, not soft-deletes or hidden retention.

Changes to this policy

If LabelProof's data practices ever change, this page will be updated before the change ships, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
